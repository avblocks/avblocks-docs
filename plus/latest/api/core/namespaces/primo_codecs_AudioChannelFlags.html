primo::codecs::AudioChannelFlags¶

Defines the speaker location of one or more audio channels.

enum primo::codecs::AudioChannelFlags::Enum¶

AudioChannelFlags::Enum.

Values:

enumerator None¶

No flag is set.

enumerator Left¶

Left.

enumerator Right¶

Right.

enumerator Center¶

Center.

enumerator LFE¶

Low-frequency effect.

Subwoofer.

enumerator BackLeft¶

Back-left.

Also known as Left Surround.

enumerator BackRight¶

Back-right.

Also known as Right Surround.

enumerator LeftCenter¶

Front, left of center.

enumerator RightCenter¶

Front, right of center.

Back-center.

Also known as Center Surround.

Side-left.
